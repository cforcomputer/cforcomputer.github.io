### Open source bed cooler

One of the things that I put off for way too long at my apartment in Groningen was getting faster internet. The main reason I didn't, was simply because I wanted to save money while spending less time in my apartment. Because sitting alone in one's apartment browsing the internet is kind of depressing.

ANYWAY. Finally I was fed up with using the shared apartment wifi. One single 100mbps connection shared between 20 people is not ideal. Oftentimes it wouldn't work at all, and I would write my book instead.

It clicked at some point that since my apartment complex was rent controlled, and attracted the kind of people who also wanted to save money, I could simply offer to split the bill between everyone on the fastest gigabit fiber available, which in this case was 4000 mbps synchronous fiber since I was in the middle of the city.

I spent several months going back and forth between the store and the dutch telecom company KPN, and they eventually did send someone out to lay a new line directly to my apartment. Then I wrote on my window advertising the internet share. Two of my neighbors signed up, and I used this as the excuse I needed to spend over a thousand dollars on ubiquiti enterprise networking gear I wanted. 

![Picture of networking setup](assets/images/projects/networking/networking-setup.webp)

I had the router, which was the Cloud Gateway Fiber running Ubiquiti Cloud OS Server, that was managing the networks I set up for my neighbors, so they could keep all of their smart devices separate.

The Network was delivered via the 6G capable UAP-AC-PRO, and everything was running through the compact 10G switch. Exceptionally overkill, but that's what I needed after decades of slow, barely functional internet. The main bottleneck ended up being my computer. I had to buy an adapter to be able to get the speeds to 4Gbit.

Cost per person? $20 per month. Everyone including me was very happy. I found that delivering a service that brought relief to people was well worth the money. I think more people could benefit from having side hustle hobbies like this that helps their communities. Just do it!
